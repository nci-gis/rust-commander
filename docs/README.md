# xdevcmd documentation

Read in this order; each page stands on its own.

1. [Guide](guide.md): what xdevcmd does, one topic at a time
2. [Keys](keys.md): every key, as shipped (F1 shows yours)
3. [Shell](shell.md): the terminal panel, and staying in the directory you quit in
4. [Config](config.md): settings, themes, bookmarks, your own commands
5. [Languages](language.md): the words on screen in another language, or one of your own
6. [Security](security.md): what xdevcmd guards against, read-only and restricted modes
7. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): a problem you see, and its fix
8. [Known issues and limits](known-issues.md): what does not work yet, and what will not change
9. [Compatibility](compatibility.md): what stays the same from 0.4.0, and what is not promised
10. [Development](development.md): build, test, release, contribute; the maintainer's
   hand tests before a pre-release
11. [How to test](how-to-test.md): see a version work on your machine, in a sandbox,
   before you trust it
12. [Benchmarks](benchmark.md): the speed on your own drives

These pages on GitHub's `main` describe the newest code, which may be ahead of yours.
**For the version you have installed, use Help → Documentation** in xdevcmd (or F1, type
"doc"): it names the copy in `docs/` beside the program, when you installed from a
release archive, and the link to these pages at your version's tag.
